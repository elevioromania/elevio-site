Make 1,000 Videos With Claude for Almost Free 🤯

What if Claude could turn a single video into fully animated, ready-to-post scenes without spending a fortune?

This plugin lets you tell Claude “let’s Flick it” and automatically transcribe your video, create a scene plan, generate animations, add matching sound effects, and turn everything into a finished video.

It runs on Remotion, the open-source video engine that turns code into real videos, and includes a live studio so you can review and tweak everything before exporting.

Perfect for creators looking to scale video production, automate content creation, and generate hundreds of videos without expensive editing tools.
