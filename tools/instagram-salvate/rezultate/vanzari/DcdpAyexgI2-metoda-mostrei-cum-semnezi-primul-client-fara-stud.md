How to sign your first AI agency client at $3-5k/mo with zero case studies.

No free trial. No discount. No begging.

Use the drug dealer method.

Every dealer on earth gives out samples. Not out of generosity — because a sample kills doubt faster than any pitch ever could.

Your problem as a beginner is simple: no proof, no trust. So your only job is moving them from zero trust to high trust, fast.

What 90% of people send: «Hey, got 30 minutes for a call? I’ve got this AI that…»

Nobody gives a stranger 30 minutes. That message dies.

Send this instead: «I already built you the AI. Can I send it over?»

Now they’re curious instead of defensive. And they’re the one asking you.

Then build the demo — personalized to their offer, their voice. Use AI to build it. Costs you 5 extra minutes. For a $3-5k/mo client.

Send the demo with your booking link.

They test it live, on their own business. Trust goes from zero to high in three messages. Now the call isn’t you begging for time — it’s them wanting to keep what they just used.

Stop trying to get people on calls. Give them something that works and let it convince them.

Proof beats persuasion.
